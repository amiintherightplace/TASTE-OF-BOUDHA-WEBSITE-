01. Welcome to Baidam Road

Nepalese Dining Rooted in Himalayan Character

Located along Baidam Road in Pokhara (Gandaki Province 33700), Taste of Boudha and Sherpa Bar welcomes local residents, trekkers returning from the Annapurna region, and evening visitors looking for unhurried Nepalese dining.

From its bamboo-framed entryway and warm woven pendant lanterns to the painted mountain ridge and traditional banners inside, the space pairs casual lakeside comfort with unmistakable Himalayan warmth.

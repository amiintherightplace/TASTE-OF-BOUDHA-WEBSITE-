About Taste of Boudha & Sherpa Bar

A Nepalese restaurant and Sherpa-inspired gathering space on Baidam Road in Pokhara, pairing familiar mountain decor with unhurried evening hospitality.

Pokhara, Gandaki Province

Baidam Road 33700, Nepal

01. Nepalese Restaurant Character

Where Himalayan Warmth Meets Pokhara Evenings

Taste of Boudha and Sherpa Bar welcomes diners along Baidam Road—the lively lakeside corridor of Pokhara where travelers, hikers, and local residents gather after days spent around Phewa Lake and the Annapurna foothills.

The restaurant's name and interior presentation draw inspiration from the cultural spirit of Boudha and Sherpa hospitality. Guests enter through a bamboo-lined threshold lit by woven pendant lanterns into an open, welcoming dining room.

02. Interior & Dining Atmosphere

Thoughtful Details Designed for Comfort

Inside the dining room, layered gray mountain silhouettes stretch across the wall beneath colorful prayer flags and traditional hanging banners.

Solid timber tables, soft cushioned seats draped with warm textured throws, and open street-facing frontage create a relaxed atmosphere—comfortable for solo travelers stopping in for a warm plate as well as groups lingering late into the evening.
